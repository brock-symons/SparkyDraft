Comms Racks
✕
Every comms/data device gets its own dedicated cable ("home run") straight back to one numbered port on a rack — separate from electrical circuits entirely. Place a Comms Rack device on the plan to get one with 24 ports (a patch panel), ready to assign. Patch panels aren't placed separately — go over 24 ports and the quote/legend automatically account for a 2nd (48 for a 3rd, and so on).
